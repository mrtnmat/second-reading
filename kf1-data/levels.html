Levels

King's Field in numbers, part 1 · revised 2026-10-24 · draft

The game's level table, as it applies when you level up. How levelling works, and why it stops at 48, is in Levels, training and the level-48 ceiling.

How to read it

  • EXP to reach is your running EXP total when the level arrives. Two thresholds passed by one kill give two levels at once.
  • Max HP / Max MP are the new maximums. Levelling up does not refill your HP.
  • STR + / MAG + are what that level adds to your base STR and MAG. From levels is the total from levels alone, starting at 20; training (one point per 100 damaging hits) and the Verdite come on top.
  • Levels marked † come from the code, not the table: the table on the disc has 50 rows, but the game loads only the first 40 and ignores the rest. Past 40 it repeats the step from level 39 to 40 (+28 HP, +18 MP, +4 STR, +4 MAG, +3,500 EXP). EXP stops at 99,999, so 48 is the last level you can reach; the next would need 100,904.
