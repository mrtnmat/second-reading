Monsters

King's Field in numbers, part 2 · revised 2026-10-24 · draft

Spoilers: the floor 4 and floor 5 tables include the late-game bosses.

King's Field has no single monster list. Each floor carries its own, so the same monster comes back re-tuned: a Mummy gives 15 EXP on floor 1, 18 on floor 2 and 26 on floor 3. The tables below are per floor, in the game's own order.

How to read it

  • On floor is how many are placed on that floor's map.
  • Gold is the range a kill can drop: any amount from 0 up to one less than the monster's gold value, all equally likely. A roll of 0 drops nothing.
  • Attack and defence come in slash, strike and thrust (sl / st / th), plus holy and fire for defence. They go into the damage formula explained in How combat really works: match your weapon to the low defence. A Skeleton's strike defence of 2 is why blunt weapons do so well against them.
  • Inflicts is the status a monster's hit can give you, and the chance on each hit that connects.
  • Drops is the item a kill may leave, with its chance. Some monsters are placed with an item of their own instead; those always drop it, and aren't listed here.
  • The game's lists carry no names. The names come from player guides, matched to the lists by floor and EXP; where the match isn't certain, the table says so.

Where the numbers come from

All values are read from each floor's data on the disc, and the code shows which field is which in the code. Checked in the running game measured:

  • EXP: the floor 1 Man Trap, Mummy and Reaper gave exactly 7, 15 and 20.
  • Attack and defence: the damage formula, fed with these values, predicted every tested hit in both directions exactly.
  • Gold and drops: 300 kills of the floor 2 Mummy dropped gold evenly between 0 and 39, never more, and the Blood Stone 25 times, in line with its 6.6 % chance.

HP is the field the code sets a monster's health from when it appears; it has not been checked kill by kill for every monster.
