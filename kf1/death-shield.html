Surviving a killing blow

King's Field, read from the code, part 7 · revised 2026-10-24 · draft

In short

In King's Field, a blow that takes your HP to zero doesn't kill you on the spot. First the view jolts for a moment, as it does for every hit, and only when that jolt is over does the game check whether you are dead. During the jolt you can still open the menu, and the game stands still while the menu is open. Use a healing item there, close the menu, and when the game finally checks, your HP isn't zero any more. You live.

Players know this trick. The Japanese speedrunner kerotto used it in their world-record speedrun work, and that is how we heard of it. It isn't a bug in the usual sense: no part of the program misbehaves. It simply falls out of the order in which the game does things, and the code lets us say exactly how big the window is, when it works and when it doesn't.

How it works

The jolt comes first

Every hit that does damage makes the view shake and tilt for 8 updates, about 0.4 seconds. The hit itself only lowers your HP, never below zero, and starts that shake. The check "is HP zero? then die" sits at the very end of the shake in the code. Without the trick, that is what happens: 8 updates at 0 HP, then the death sequence measured.

The menu still opens

While the view is shaking, the game keeps reading the controller as usual. Pressing the menu button opens the menu, and while the menu is open nothing in the dungeon moves. Healing items and the healing spells cast from the menu change your HP immediately in the code. When you close the menu, the shake carries on where it was; at its end the game looks at your HP, finds it above zero, and you carry on playing measured.

We tested it in the running game: a deadly hit with a Dragon Grass Fruit in the bag. The menu opened with the status panel reading "HP 0", the Fruit restored 300 HP, and the shake ended without a death measured.

How long you have

The menu can be opened on 7 of the shake's 8 updates, a little over a third of a second; a press on the last one is too late, because the death has already begun measured. The menu takes a moment to load its pages before it reacts to buttons, but the game is frozen meanwhile, so that costs nothing.

When it doesn't work

  • In the middle of your own swing. The menu won't open while your weapon swing is still running. If the killing blow lands while you are attacking, you can't reach the menu in time measured.
  • Deaths that don't come from a hit. Poison, items that drain your HP, and falling into a pit kill you at once, with no shake and no window in the code.

A string of hits

Each new hit restarts the shake from the beginning in the code. So if blows keep landing, the deadly check keeps being pushed back: you die 8 updates after the last hit, not the first. The more often you are being hit, the more time you have to reach the menu. We read this in the code but haven't watched it with a monster yet in the code.

The details

The order inside one update

kf_PlayerUpdate (80018880) runs once per update:

  1. Return early into the death camera (state −1) or the fade-in after revival (−2); otherwise:
  2. Read the pad. A new Cross (or Select) press with the swing timer 800a07f0 idle (0xFFFF) calls the menu (kf_Menu_guess(0) → kf_MenuMain), which loops on its own until it closes in the code.
  3. Later: if the camera state 800a0822 is 1–7, write that step's tilt and add one; at 8, clear the tilt and set 0, and call kf_PlayerDie only if HP is 0 in the code.

The hit routine (FUN_80016324) subtracts the damage, floors HP at 0 and sets the camera state to 1 unless it is −1; it never calls kf_PlayerDie itself in the code. Healing writes HP directly: from Use item (Herb +25, Antidote Herb +10, Recovery Potion +80, Dragon Grass Leaf +150, Dragon Grass Fruit +300) or from Magic (Healing +MAG, Bless +3 × MAG), capped at the maximum in the code. Poison, drain and regeneration items go through kf_AddHP_guess, which calls kf_PlayerDie directly at HP 0; pits call it from kf_UpdatePlayerHeight in the code.

The run

Mednafen, 2026-10-24: floor 1, the damaging floor tile at (90,28), maximum HP 900, HP 5, one Dragon Grass Fruit. The hit, the menu and the item are the game's own; only the starting position, HP and bag were set up measured.
