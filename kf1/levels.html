Levels, training and the level-48 ceiling

King's Field, read from the code, part 5 · revised 2026-10-23 · draft

In short

King's Field has two ways of growing stronger, and the code settles exactly how both work:

  • Levels come from experience points (EXP). The game carries a table for levels 1 to 40, but it doesn't stop there: past 40 it keeps adding the table's last step again and again. The real limit is the experience counter, which can't go above 99,999. That makes level 48 the highest you can reach.
  • Training raises STR and MAG by practice: every 100 hits that actually do damage add one point. Misses don't count, and neither do hits that do nothing, but the killing blow does.

Your class title on the status screen (SOLDIER, MAGE, SWORDMASTER…) is worked out from your base STR and MAG each time the screen is drawn. And the "total attack" and "total defence" numbers on that screen are for show: combat never uses them.

How it works

Levelling up

Each monster gives a fixed amount of EXP when it dies: the floor-1 Man Trap 7, a Mummy 15, a Reaper 20 measured. Your EXP is a running total. When it passes the next threshold, the game copies that level's row from its table: new maximum HP and MP, plus a gain to STR and MAG measured. Two thresholds passed at once give two levels at once measured. A level's gain can be zero: going from level 3 to 4 adds 2 STR and no MAG measured.

Levelling up does not refill your HP measured.

Past level 40

The table has 40 rows. From level 40 on, each new level adds the difference between the last two rows: +28 max HP, +18 max MP, +4 STR, +4 MAG, and the next threshold 3,500 EXP further on measured. With EXP capped at 99,999, the last threshold you can pass is the one for level 48 in the code. Earlier notes, including our own, said the game stops at 40; that was wrong, and was corrected when the level-41 step was measured.

Training by practice

Every hit that does damage to a living creature adds one to a hidden counter measured:

  • a hit with slash, strike or thrust damage (a weapon) counts toward STR;
  • a hit with none of those (a holy or fire spell) counts toward MAG.

Every 100 counts gives one point of the base stat, and your stats are recalculated at once measured. A missed swing, a hit that does zero damage and a missed spell add nothing measured. Healing and other support spells cast from the menu train nothing either in the code. The killing blow counts, because the creature still had HP when it was hit measured.

The Verdite, an item that raises MAG by one, works by adding 100 to the MAG counter. That gives the point, but the counter then resets to zero, so any MAG training you had built up is lost measured.

Class titles

The title on the status screen is picked from a 3 × 3 grid by your base STR and MAG, each in three bands (under 40, 40–59, 60 and up) measured:

MAG under 40MAG 40–59MAG 60+
STR under 40SOLDIERMAGEWIZARD
STR 40–59HIGHSOLDIERMAGEFIGHTERWIZARD
STR 60+SWORDMANSWORDMANSWORDMASTER

Two of the nine cells repeat a title, so there are seven different titles. The cells STR 40–59 with MAG 60+, and STR 60+ with MAG 40–59, follow from the code; the other seven were checked on screen in the code. The title is never stored; it is computed while the screen is drawn. Because it uses base stats, equipment like the Blood Mask (−8 STR) doesn't change your title in the code.

Learning spells by MAG

Some spells come from magic power itself: above 36 MAG (if you know Healing) you learn Dispoison, above 69 Fire Wall, above 74 Lightning Bolt in the code.

The status screen's totals

The status screen shows a "total attack" (総合攻撃力) and a "total defence" (総合防御力). They are weighted sums of your attack and defence values, made for display only; nothing in combat reads them in the code. The way damage is really worked out is in How combat really works.

The details

Level-up

FUN_80016058, called with each kill's EXP: while EXP (800a0780, u32, capped at 99,999) ≥ the threshold (800a0784), copy the new level's row from the table at 800650b0 (12-byte rows: max HP, max MP, STR gain, MAG gain, EXP threshold) into max HP/MP, add the gains to base STR (800a07a2) and MAG (800a07a4), and increment the level (800a0788). From index 40 on, it adds row 39 − row 38 and row 39's gains instead. Max HP/MP cap at 9,999; base STR/MAG at 999 in the code.

After level 41 the thresholds run 79,904, 83,404, 86,904, 90,404, 93,904 and 97,404 (level 48); the next would be 100,904, above the cap.

EXP per kill is the monster type record's 16-bit value at +0x84 (records at 8006bd98 + 0x98 × type) measured.

Training

kf_DamageEnemy calls FUN_80015f28 (STR) or FUN_80015fc0 (MAG) for every damaging player hit (source byte & 0xF0 == 0x10) on a creature with HP > 0. Counters at 800a079e (STR) and 800a07a0 (MAG); at 100: +1 base stat, counter to 0, stats recalculated. These functions have exactly two callers, kf_DamageEnemy and kf_UseItem (80018054, the Verdite: counter += 100) in the code. Measured from counter 99: one sword hit → STR 20 → 21, counter 0; one Fire Ball hit → MAG 20 → 21 measured.

One branch is still open: in the decompiled code, a hit with slash but no strike and no thrust would count toward neither stat. No weapon has that profile; whether a spell does is unmeasured in the code.

Class

The status screens (FUN_800264d8, FUN_80025f38): index = MAG tier + 3 × STR tier (tiers at 40 and 60, from base stats), drawn as four glyph cells at 0x100 + 4 × index of the COM/MIX.TIM sheet. Captured on screen for (STR, MAG) 20/20, 39/39, 40/20, 59/20, 20/40, 20/59, 60/20, 20/60, 40/40, 60/60, both sides of each band edge measured. WIZARD is drawn in red, SWORDMAN in yellow-green, SWORDMASTER in blue, the rest in white.

Totals and healing spells

  • Total attack = (((slash + strike + thrust) · 3 >> 1) + (holy + fire) · 2) · 10 >> 3; the Short Sword gives 7, as on screen. Total defence = (slash + strike + thrust + poison/5 + dark + fire) · 10 / 7, with slash already doubled in the code.
  • Menu-cast healing (FUN_8002317c): Healing HP += MAG; Dispoison clears poison, paralysis and the Resist Fire buff; Resist Fire sets its buff; Bless clears curse, dark and the buff and adds 3 × MAG HP. HP is capped at the maximum in the code.
  • Spell learning flags at 8009ce60 + 0x14 × spell, set when MAG passes 36 (with Healing known), 69 and 74 in the code.
