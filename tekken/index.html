Tekken, read from the code

draft

Tekken was Namco's 3D fighting game, first in arcades and then, in March 1995, on the PlayStation in Japan. Two fighters, a ring and a life bar each: simple on the surface, with a lot of hidden machinery underneath.

We have been taking its program apart, and much of that machinery now runs in our own copy and matches the original exactly, step by step. This series explains what we found. Each article starts in plain language and gets more technical as it goes; stop wherever you like.

  1. 1How the computer chooses its moves · draft

    The computer opponent in Tekken doesn't get special powers. It "holds a controller" like you do, makes a fresh choice every other frame from the moves its fighter could do right now, and rolls dice that are tilted by difficulty, by its strength level and by how the life bars compare.
